PAYMENTS AND TRANSACTIONS GUIDE - BFSI Banking Corp

NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer)
NEFT operates in half-hourly batches from {neft.operating_hours} on weekdays.
No transaction on bank holidays and Sundays.
Minimum amount: INR 1. No maximum limit.
Charges: Up to {neft.slab1_max} - {neft.slab1_charge}. {neft.slab1_max} to {neft.slab2_max} - {neft.slab2_charge}. Above {neft.slab2_max} - {neft.slab3_charge}.
Settlement: Not real-time, settled in batches.

RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement)
RTGS available from {rtgs.operating_hours} on weekdays.
Minimum amount: {rtgs.min_amount}. No maximum limit.
Used for high-value transactions. Real-time settlement.
Charges: {rtgs.min_amount} to {rtgs.slab1_max} - {rtgs.slab1_charge}. Above {rtgs.slab1_max} - {rtgs.slab2_charge}.

IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)
IMPS is available 24x7 including holidays.
Minimum amount: INR 1. Maximum: {imps.max_per_transaction} per transaction.
Real-time transfer using MMID or account number + IFSC.
Charges: {imps.charge} flat for transactions up to {imps.charge_limit}.

UPI (Unified Payments Interface)
UPI transactions are free for personal use.
Maximum per transaction: {upi.max_per_transaction}.
Daily limit: {upi.daily_limit} per UPI ID.
Refund for failed UPI transactions: Within {upi.refund_days} business days.

TRANSACTION LIMITS
Cash deposit at branch: No limit, but above {cash.pan_required_above} requires PAN.
ATM daily withdrawal limit: {atm.classic_daily_limit} for Classic card, {atm.platinum_daily_limit} for Platinum.
International transactions: Disabled by default, can be enabled via mobile app.
Suspicious transaction threshold: Any single transaction above {cash.suspicious_threshold} triggers reporting.
