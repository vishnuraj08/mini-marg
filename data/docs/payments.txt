PAYMENTS AND TRANSACTIONS GUIDE - BFSI Banking Corp

NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer)
NEFT operates in half-hourly batches from 8 AM to 7 PM on weekdays.
No transaction on bank holidays and Sundays.
Minimum amount: INR 1. No maximum limit.
Charges: Up to INR 10,000 - INR 2.50. INR 10,001 to INR 1 lakh - INR 5. Above INR 1 lakh - INR 15.
Settlement: Not real-time, settled in batches.

RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement)
RTGS available from 7 AM to 6 PM on weekdays.
Minimum amount: INR 2,00,000. No maximum limit.
Used for high-value transactions. Real-time settlement.
Charges: INR 2 lakh to INR 5 lakh - INR 25. Above INR 5 lakh - INR 50.

IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)
IMPS is available 24x7 including holidays.
Minimum amount: INR 1. Maximum: INR 5,00,000 per transaction.
Real-time transfer using MMID or account number + IFSC.
Charges: INR 5 flat for transactions up to INR 1 lakh.

UPI (Unified Payments Interface)
UPI transactions are free for personal use.
Maximum per transaction: INR 1,00,000 (INR 2,00,000 for certain categories).
Daily limit: INR 1,00,000 per UPI ID.
Refund for failed UPI transactions: Within 5 business days.

TRANSACTION LIMITS
Cash deposit at branch: No limit, but above INR 50,000 requires PAN.
ATM daily withdrawal limit: INR 40,000 for Classic card, INR 1,00,000 for Platinum.
International transactions: Disabled by default, can be enabled via mobile app.
Suspicious transaction threshold: Any single transaction above INR 10 lakh triggers reporting.
