LOANS PRODUCT GUIDE - BFSI Banking Corp

PERSONAL LOANS
Personal loans are unsecured loans offered to salaried and self-employed individuals.
Minimum loan amount: {personal_loan.min_amount}. Maximum loan amount: {personal_loan.max_amount}.
Interest rate: {personal_loan.interest_rate_min} to {personal_loan.interest_rate_max} per annum depending on credit score.
Tenure: {personal_loan.tenure_min} to {personal_loan.tenure_max} months.
Processing fee: {personal_loan.processing_fee} of loan amount + GST.
Eligibility: Minimum age {personal_loan.min_age} years, maximum age {personal_loan.max_age} years at loan maturity.

HOME LOANS
Home loans are secured loans against residential property.
Minimum loan amount: {home_loan.min_amount}. Maximum: No upper limit subject to property valuation.
Interest rate: {home_loan.interest_rate_min} to {home_loan.interest_rate_max} per annum. Floating rate linked to MCLR.
Tenure: Up to {home_loan.max_tenure_years} years.
Processing fee: {home_loan.processing_fee_pct} of loan amount maximum {home_loan.processing_fee_max}.
LTV (Loan to Value): Up to {home_loan.ltv_above_75L} of property value for loans above INR 75 lakhs.
Prepayment: No penalty on floating rate loans.

GOLD LOANS
Gold loans are secured against gold ornaments and coins.
Interest rate: {gold_loan.interest_rate_min} to {gold_loan.interest_rate_max} per annum.
Loan to value: Up to {gold_loan.ltv} of gold value as per RBI guidelines.
Tenure: {gold_loan.tenure_min_months} to {gold_loan.tenure_max_months} months.
Processing fee: {gold_loan.processing_fee} of loan amount.
Repayment: Bullet payment or monthly EMI option available.

CREDIT SCORE IMPACT
Credit score above {credit_score.excellent_threshold}: Best interest rates, faster approval.
Credit score {credit_score.good_min}-{credit_score.good_max}: Standard rates, normal processing.
Credit score {credit_score.average_min}-{credit_score.average_max}: Higher rates, additional collateral may be required.
Credit score below {credit_score.poor_threshold}: Loan may be rejected or requires guarantor.

EMI CALCULATION
EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1)
Where P = Principal, r = monthly interest rate, n = number of months.
Example: INR 5,00,000 personal loan at 12% for 36 months = EMI of INR 16,607.
