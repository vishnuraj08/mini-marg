KYC (KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER) GUIDELINES - BFSI Banking Corp

KYC REQUIREMENTS
KYC is mandatory for all bank account holders as per RBI regulations.
KYC must be completed within {kyc.completion_months} months of account opening for new customers.
Existing customers must re-KYC every {kyc.high_risk_years} years for high-risk, {kyc.medium_risk_years} years for medium-risk, {kyc.low_risk_years} years for low-risk.

DOCUMENTS ACCEPTED FOR KYC
Identity Proof (any one): PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License.
Address Proof (any one): Aadhaar Card, Passport, Utility Bill (not older than 3 months), Bank Statement.
PAN Card is mandatory for transactions above {cash.pan_required_above}.
Aadhaar-based e-KYC is accepted for digital onboarding.

VIDEO KYC (V-KYC)
Video KYC allows customers to complete KYC remotely via video call.
Customer must show original documents during video call.
Available {kyc.vkyc_days}, {kyc.vkyc_hours}.
V-KYC is valid equivalent to in-person KYC as per RBI guidelines.

KYC STATUS AND CONSEQUENCES
Pending KYC: Account will be restricted after {kyc.completion_months} months - only credits allowed, no debits.
Expired KYC: Account frozen until KYC is updated.
KYC rejection reasons: Blurry documents, mismatch in name/address, expired documents.

AML (ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING)
Transactions above {aml.cash_report_threshold} in cash must be reported to FIU-IND.
Suspicious transaction reports filed for unusual patterns.
FATF high-risk countries list updated quarterly - enhanced due diligence required.
PEP (Politically Exposed Persons) require additional verification and senior approval.

CKYC (CENTRAL KYC)
CKYC registry maintained by CERSAI stores KYC records centrally.
Once CKYC is done with one institution, customers can use CKYC number for other banks.
CKYC reduces paperwork and speeds up onboarding across financial institutions.
